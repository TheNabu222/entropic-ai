# Unsorted Cavebot Sorting Guide

This folder contains 558 files: 553 PNG images and 5 MOV files.

Open `UNSORTED_ASSET_REVIEW.html` to assign each asset:

1. a primary destination category
2. a fate: production, archive, or delete

The page stores decisions in browser local storage and exports `cavebot-unsorted-decisions.json`.

## Why These Categories

The folder includes all of the following:

- isolated characters and relationship pairs
- Anzu, shoebills, ravens, owls, eagles, and divine birds
- animals, familiars, fantasy creatures, and fossils
- landscapes, interiors, ziggurats, temples, and backrooms
- already-composited gameplay and story scenes
- dialogue frames, HUD pieces, status symbols, and interface mockups
- inventory props, offerings, flowers, tools, vessels, and artifacts
- symbolic icons, hearts, brains, eyes, trees, hands, and recursive marks
- maps, tablets, bestiaries, diagrams, and skill trees
- lore cards, screenshots, articles, text panels, and faux archives
- finished card/deck assets
- computers, terminals, corruption messages, containment systems, and cybernetic material
- later-act recursion, Panopticon, Babel, nuclear, and backrooms imagery
- real-world screenshots and inspiration material

The goal is not to homogenize Cavebot's visual language. The goal is to distinguish usable game material from references, experiments, duplicates, and future-act assets.
