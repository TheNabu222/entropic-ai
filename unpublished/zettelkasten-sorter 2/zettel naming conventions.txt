The Only Rule You Really Need
Just build the address “as you think of it.” Example stream-of-consciousness:
	•	Start with main topic:  1000 
	•	“Hermetics?” →  1000/1 
	•	“Subprinciple?” →  1000/1-A 
	•	“Side note?” →  1000/1-A.1 
	•	“Wait, a weird bud/exception?” →  1000/1-A.1a 
	•	“An even weirder draft annex?” →  1000/1-A.1a{draft} 
If you get lost:
	•	Pick a “parent” card, add a dot/letter/whatever, name the child.
You don’t have to remember whether to use a dash or dot or slash “properly”—just be consistent within that parent/child chain so it’s easy for you to tell which card “grows out of” which.
Your Real-World Indexing Thought Process
Ask yourself, for any new card:
	1.	“Is this a main department? (topic)” → Give it a fresh  1000 ,  13000 , etc.
	2.	“Is this an offshoot of a topic?” → Use  / ,  - , or  .  to nest it from its parent.
