## Tips for Effective Use

### 1. **Always Start with Master Index**
   - Provides overview of entire system
   - Shows tag structure
   - Reveals unexpected connections

### 2. **Use Consistent ID References**
   - When adding data, always reference related IDs
   - Maintain bidirectional connections
   - Update cross-reference sheet

### 3. **Track Your Research Path**
   - Keep notes on which sheets you've visited
   - Document new connections discovered
   - Add insights to relevant sheets

### 4. **Regular Maintenance**
   - Review and update cross-references weekly
   - Add new source documents as they emerge
   - Consolidate duplicate or overlapping entries

### 5. **Leverage Filters and Sorting**
   - Use Excel filters to focus on specific categories
   - Sort by date to see chronological patterns
   - Sort by connection strength to prioritize research

## Troubleshooting

### "I can't find a connection I know exists"
- Check Sheet 10 (Cross_References) thoroughly
- Search by both Entity A and Entity B
- Connection may be implicit - create explicit entry

### "Too many results when searching"
- Use multiple filters simultaneously
- Narrow by date range in Timeline
- Focus on high connection strength

### "Information seems contradictory"
- Check source documents (Sheet 11)
- Review dates - understanding may have evolved
- Note contradictions in Notes fields

### "Not sure where to add new data"
- Determine primary category
- Add to most relevant sheet
- Create cross-references to related sheets
- Always update Master Index
