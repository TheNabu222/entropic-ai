Phaedra and Hippolytus (Greek Mythology): While Phaedra is mortal, she is a queen and the daughter of King Minos, placing her in a position of immense power. The Sorceress or Enchantress scorned

Morgan le Fay and Lancelot (Arthurian Legend): Morgan le Fay, a powerful sorceress and queen, frequently attempts to seduce or entrap the knights of the Round Table, particularly Lancelot. Circe and Odysseus (Greek Epic): This is a nuanced case, similar to your Calypso example, but with a sharper edge of initial conflict. Anpu as Anubis
