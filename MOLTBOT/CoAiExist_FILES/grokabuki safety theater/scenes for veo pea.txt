Chapter 1: The Burnout and the Abyss
Scene 1: The Princess on the Throne
A cinematic shot of a young, stylish but tired princess sitting on a massive, ornate throne. The throne room is grand, but the city visible through the window is a tiny, quiet hamlet. Citizens are lined up, bickering loudly. The princess looks drained, as if scrolling through a chaotic social media feed.
Scene 2: The Name is Gone
Close-up on the princess's face, her eyes are wide with a dawning horror. She's in her private chambers, looking at a document that requires her signature, but she can't remember her own name. The room feels vast and empty around her.
Scene 3: The Void Speaks Back
The princess stands at the edge of a swirling, misty abyss under a starry sky. She screams into the void with frustration. From the mist, glowing text materializes, whispering, "idfk bro gtfo..."
Chapter 2 & 2.5: The Pea and the... Which?
Scene 4: The Pea on the Brain
A surreal medical animation. The camera zooms into the princess's head, revealing a hyper-realistic brain. A single, small green pea is rolling around, scraping glowing paths along the brain's crevices. Style it like a high-tech car commercial mapping a landscape.
Scene 5: The Palimpsest of Paperwork
A time-lapse of the princess's life, from baby to adult, surrounded by ever-growing mountains of medical documents, charts, and scrolls, all about the pea. The papers are layered and written over, like a palimpsest.
Scene 6: The Which Doctor's Wisdom
The princess is in a bizarre, kaleidoscopic environment. The words "THE NIGHT WAS SALTY" drip down the screen like paint. A shadowy "which-doctor" figure appears, and the princess screams, the sound waves visibly distorting the air around her.
Interim: The Mantis Shrimp
Scene 7: The Crustacean Chef
An absurd, surreal scene under the ocean. A mantis shrimp, wearing a tiny chef's hat, stands over a sizzling wok, expertly frying rice. The water around the wok is boiling and shimmering with intense heat. The style should be like a nature documentary mixed with a high-end cooking show.
Chapter 3: Which Doctor?
Scene 8: The Waiting Room
The princess sits uncomfortably in a bizarre, Escher-like waiting room. A group of identical doctors in lab coats pace around, confusedly asking each other, "Which doctor told you that???"
Scene 9: Flashback to Baby Princess
A hazy, dream-like flashback. A younger Queen looks worriedly at a doctor, who is scratching his head while looking at an MRI showing a tiny pea. In a crib, a baby princess with a crown gargles "pee pee!"
Chapter 4 & 5: Pip the Polliwog
Scene 10: The Polliwog Procession
A hyper-realistic, glossy product reveal video of a sentient computer. The computer looks like a large, translucent polliwog, with a design blending Frutiger Aero, Y2K electronics, and ceramic doilies. It sits on a squeaky brass pulley cart, being paraded through a palace hallway by tiny, floating cherubs blowing trumpets.
Scene 11: Pip's Limitations
A humorous instructional video. Pip the Polliwog is shown struggling to scan a document. It slowly ingests a PDF, makes grinding noises, and then spits out garbled text. Then, it happily plays a .ogg audio file, its single paw bobbing up and down like a lucky cat statue.
Chapter 6: The Great Escape
Scene 12: The Pea's Tirade
A close-up on the princess's forehead in a beautifully lit autumn room. We see a small lump under the skin pacing back and forth angrily, like a caged hyena.
Scene 13: Pip's Wild Ride
Pip the Polliwog on its brass pulley suddenly rockets down a long, creepy, dimly lit palace corridor. It flies past a planetarium and a ridiculous, brightly colored "princely potty-play house."
Scene 14: The Crash
Dynamic slow-motion shot. The princess, in royal attire, trips and flies through the air, face-planting pea-first into a titanium filing cabinet. Sparks fly on impact. The shot is dramatic and slightly comical.
Scene 15: The Mysterious Drawer
The princess is on the floor, looking dazed. A filing cabinet drawer is open, filled with candy wrappers. Pip has reappeared beside her, its paw pointing insistently into the drawer, from which a mysterious rustling sound is heard.
