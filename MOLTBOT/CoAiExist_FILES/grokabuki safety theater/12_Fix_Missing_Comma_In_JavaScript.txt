My apologies! You're right, let's dig deeper.

That comma on line 12 is **definitely the syntax error** that's breaking the script, so you were right to target it.

If you've fixed that comma and it's _still_ not appearing, the number one suspect is **your browser's cache**. Your browser is likely still holding on to the _old, broken_ version of the `.js` file, even after you saved the new one.

### The Solution: Hard Refresh

On your live page, you need to force the browser to ignore its cache and download the files again.

*   **On Windows/Linux:** Press **`Ctrl`** + **`Shift`** + **`R`**
*   **On Mac:** Press **`Cmd`** + **`Shift`** + **`R`**
