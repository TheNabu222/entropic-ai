Cross-continental pattern
Despite zero historical contact, the I-N-N-A nucleus surfaces in female sovereignty / sky / mother / first-born semantics across Siberia, Japan, sub-Saharan Africa, and the Americas. The seed “Inanna” behaves as a global phonetic icon for the archetype of the elevated woman.
